# Storyboard — 44 chapters, 6 movements

Target: ~101,000 words of script ≈ 11h30m of narration, plus ~15 min of titles, chapter cards and music breaths. Each chapter targets **~2,300 words ≈ 16 minutes**. Every chapter must either grow Umbrella or grow him — ideally both — and must leave a side character changed.

Legend: ◆ System event · ▲ threshold (split-personality spike) · ✎ side-character turn

---

## MOVEMENT I — THE GARAGE EMPIRE (Sep 2026 – Mar 2027)

### 1. Two Deaths and a Chair
- Cold open: a bunker, 1945 — a single shot in the dark; the narrator names what died. Then a volcano in another universe, a rocket, a scream of fire: a second man ends. Two deaths that never met.
- 14 Sep 2026, 3:12 a.m. Rain. An office chair in Division Seven. He wakes. The body answers too fast; a coffee mug caught before it hits the floor.
- Mirror in the washroom: blond, slit pupils flaring. Two memories fighting for the steering wheel. They do not merge; they sit down across from each other.
- ◆ The System opens: Host confirmed. Stats. Viral Stability 71% (decaying). Mission: **The Director's Chair**.
- Walter Pruitt, night guard, knocks. First aura-farm, by instinct. ✎ Walter decides the new director is "a different animal."
- The cheap office PC. Adolf reads his own verdict: the war, the camps, the millions, the ashes. Narrator is unflinching. Adolf feels no remorse — only the insult of defeat. Wesker: *"You lost because they saw you coming."* The thesis.
- End: dawn over the Christina River. He sets the sunglasses on. "Then they will not see me coming."

### 2. Payroll
- Morning. The staff arrive: Dana, Lessing, Evelyn, Tomás, Nadia (Walter goes home). The parent company, Umbrella Holdings Ltd (UK), is in administration. Friday's payroll will bounce.
- First staff meeting in the break room. He listens. He learns each of them in a sentence. ✎ Evelyn notices he read her ignored paper.
- The asset: the freezer archive — thirty years of odd cell lines. Verdant Axis wants it; a Chinese CRO has asked. The administrators (Harrow & Lisle, London) want the estate closed.
- The play: sell Verdant Axis a *non-exclusive* license to the archive for $1.2M; use it to buy Division Seven — name, trademark, lease, IP — out of the estate for $400k in a management buyout. Payroll made Thursday night.
- ◆ Mission complete: *The Director's Chair*. Points: +1,500. "Acquisition: Umbrella. The name is yours." Level 2.
- ▲ small: Adolf wants the staff to know who saved them. Wesker lets Dana announce it. Credit is currency; he spends it where it buys most.

### 3. The First Purchase
- The Shop opens: the catalogue in full (facilities, personnel, powers, immunities, blueprints). He wants everything. The stability meter reads 66%.
- ◆ Buys **Prototype Stabilization I** — humility, once. And **Comptroller "Mr. Pell"** — arrives Monday as if hired months ago, with a CPA license from three states.
- Lessing finds anomalies in a blood sample Tomás ran "for calibration" (Wesker's). He knows what he has seen and wants it. ✎ Lessing starts copying files.
- Wesker's first night in the lab: he runs the Prototype under the microscope himself. The descending four-note motif on the detuned break-room piano (Tomás's joke instrument).
- End: Pell, reading the ledger aloud, flat: "There is a problem with the landlord."

### 4. Fate, or Variance
- Points from Pell's first week (refinanced debt, clawed-back UK receivables): enough for pulls. Adolf calls it fate; Wesker calls it variance.
- ◆ Gacha: Common — **Gus, Maintenance Technician** (arrives with his own keys). Rare — **Microexpression Cold-Read**. Then gold light: Legendary — **Tyrant, Series T-00, cryo-cradle**. For half a second he is afraid. Then he is in love with the feeling.
- The cradle manifests in the basement as a hulking "HVAC unit" with a service tag. Its breathing is a compressor cycle.
- Kuchar the landlord notices the power draw and the new unit. Wants to inspect.
- End: Wesker alone in the basement, hand on frosted glass. Something inside opens its eyes.

### 5. The Landlord, the Inspector, and the Reporter
- Kuchar: bought out through a Pell LLC in 48 hours at a price he can't refuse, with a clause he didn't read.
- ◆ **Security Chief Hale** (summoned). The first human staff meet him. He doesn't blink enough. ✎ Dana starts saying "Mr. Hale" and doesn't know why.
- Delgado the state inspector: charmed, papered, given a real safety improvement to put in his report.
- Hannah Price (*Wilmington Ledger*) covers the collapse of Umbrella Holdings. Coffee with Wesker. She is sharp; he is sharper. He gives her a better story: Verdant Axis's CEO, Tolliver, has a problem in his Phase II data.
- ◆ +Points: acting intelligently.

### 6. The Leak
- Lessing's preprint draft to *bioRxiv* and an email to a Verdant Axis VP. Cold-Read makes it obvious at Monday's meeting.
- ▲ Adolf wants an example. Wesker wants an asset. Night, the lab, the glasses come off. Lessing sees the eyes. A choice offered in a quiet voice.
- Lessing deletes the draft himself, with Wesker watching. ✎ The first man converted by fear. He will never be the same, and he will never be loyal — only frightened. (Seed for Ch 18.)
- Wesker wins the argument in the car. Adolf: "You have made a man who hates you." Wesker: "I have made a man who obeys me. Hate is a hobby."

### 7. Sermon in a Break Room
- Money exists now. Raises. New equipment. Tomás teaches Wesker TikTok, X, LinkedIn, YouTube: the 2026 crowd. Adolf is fascinated; the crowd is still the crowd, only faster.
- The Friday speech in the break room: five minutes, no notes. Frightened, underpaid technicians become believers. ◆ Morale reward, the biggest yet. Level 5. **Command Presence I**.
- ◆ Blueprint: **Rapid Pathogen Assay**. The first clean product — a biosecurity test for labs and hospitals. Evelyn leads it. ✎ Evelyn's worship begins.
- Nadia notices the speech too, and doesn't clap.

### 8. Verdant Axis
- Tolliver strikes: a lawsuit over the "exclusive" archive; poaching offers to Evelyn and Tomás; a whisper campaign.
- Hannah's article lands: Verdant Axis's Phase II irregularities. The stock falls 60% in a day.
- Wesker needs capital for a takeover. The hedge fund Keel Harbour's analyst, Catherine Adeyemi, reads his filings and calls him: "You wrote that article." He doesn't deny it. She finances the bridge — on her terms. ✎ The CFO who can doubt.
- Tolliver's counter: he sends men to look at the basement at night. Hale and Gus. Nobody is hurt. The men quit the next morning and will not say why.

### 9. The Name on the Door
- The Verdant Axis board meeting. Wesker arrives last. Says eleven words. Takes off the glasses. Tolliver resigns in the room.
- ◆ Corporate conquest: Points flood. Level 10. Ten-pull: **Arklay Sublevel** (concealed BSL-4 facility), **Silent Security Squad x6**, two Commons, and a sealed **Progenitor Seed** marked *NOT YET*.
- The new sign goes up: the red-and-white umbrella. Hannah photographs it and feels cold.
- Drive home: banners vs. deniability, the whole argument. Adolf: "A people that believes." Wesker: "A world that signs." They agree on only one thing: *more*.
- End of Movement I card.

## MOVEMENT II — THE COMPANY THAT SHOULD NOT EXIST (Apr 2027 – Dec 2028)

### 10. The Clean Face
- Umbrella Life Sciences, Inc. — Catherine builds the public company; Pell builds the shells beneath it (Delaware, Nevada, Ireland, Singapore).
- Arklay Sublevel manifests beneath the campus: a freight elevator that officially goes nowhere.
- The Silent Ones: cameras glitch around them. Walter notices; Walter says nothing.
- ✎ Catherine sets one condition: she wants to know where the money truly goes. He agrees and lies to her beautifully.

### 11. Aeterna
- ◆ Blueprint: **Cellular Renewal Compound** → *Aeterna*, a longevity therapy that works. Fast-tracked trials (legitimately astonishing data).
- The first TV interview: CNBC-style fictional network. The sunglasses, the eleven-second silence, the clip that goes viral. "Sunglasses CEO." Tomás engineers the myth. ▲ Adolf discovers he loves a world of cameras.
- IPO. The bell. ◆ Points.

### 12. The Black Interior
- Evelyn, promoted, is shown the Sublevel and the Tyrant. She is not afraid. ✎ "It's beautiful." The Progenitor Initiative is born.
- Nadia sees a shipment manifest she shouldn't. Catherine finds the black budget line.
- Confrontation: Catherine vs. Wesker in the glass office. She stays. Her price: truth. He tells her a third of it.

### 13. The Recruiter
- Wesker recruits across the world: Oxford, Basel, Singapore, Tel Aviv, Toronto. Money, flattery, fear. A Nobel laureate says no, then yes.
- ◆ Summoned research cohort x12: perfect scientists who never get tired. ◆ Gacha: **Thought Acceleration I**.
- Human vs. summoned: the humans notice the summoned never go home.

### 14. The Story Can Smell You Back
- Hannah's follow-up: Lessing talks to her anonymously. A missing Verdant Axis security contractor.
- Wesker invites her in: an exclusive profile, full access (to the clean face). She accepts to get inside.
- A dinner scene. Two predators. He tells her one true thing to make the rest believable.

### 15. Nadia
- Nadia assembles evidence: shipment logs, animal-facility records, a Sublevel photo.
- Wesker knows. He lets her go. "Let them come. On my terms." Adolf thinks he's mad.
- Nadia walks out into the rain. She files with the CDC and the FDA. ✎ The principled one leaves; the story follows her.

### 16. The Creed
- Global expansion: a UK lab (Umbrella's British roots), a Swiss sample vault, a Singapore plant.
- ◆ Level 20. **Boardroom Gravity**.
- Adolf wants a movement; Wesker gives him a corporate culture: the Umbrella Creed, the internal oath, the uniforms in the labs. Morale explodes. ◆
- End: a federal subpoena on Dana's desk. Signed: Owen Carrick, FBI. End of Movement II.

## MOVEMENT III — THE AGENCIES (2029 – 2030)

### 17. Special Agent Carrick
- Carrick: Philadelphia field office, WMD coordinator, careerist, one promotion away from Washington.
- Dr. Samira Haddad (CDC EIS) investigating Nadia's complaint. Joint visit. The clean-face tour. Wesker's hospitality is a weapon.
- Carrick notices one thing: the security guards don't blink.

### 18. Chain of Custody
- Legal warfare. Umbrella's lawyers are faster than the government's. Catherine's filings bury Carrick in documents.
- Carrick finds Lessing. ✎ Lessing becomes an FBI informant.
- Cold-Read tells Wesker the day it happens. He does nothing. Adolf is appalled. Wesker: "Let him wear the wire."

### 19. The Ghost Video
- A night break-in: a contractor team hired by a rival (money traced to Halvorsen Pike, later) to steal the Aeterna master cell bank. Lessing let them in.
- Wesker is there. Three men die in forty seconds. One in the parking lot — a passing driver's dashcam catches a blur.
- ▲ **The first killing that cannot be hidden.** Lessing dies in the chaos (the intruders shoot him). Adolf exults. Wesker is furious at the loss of deniability — then turns it: self-defense; heroic CEO; victim of corporate espionage.
- The "ghost video" goes viral. ◆ **Immunity: Mundane Disease & Toxins**. Carrick watches frame 1,114 two hundred times. His obsession begins.

### 20. The Hunt
- Carrick builds a case that sounds insane. His boss starts to worry about him.
- CIA's Martin Sloane appears, interested in the "tech".
- Wesker turns investigators into scandals: a budget story about Carrick's office; Haddad's CDC report buried in HHS politics.
- Hannah's profile publishes: glowing, with one knife-line at the end. ◆ **Memory Palace**, **Multilingual Competence**.

### 21. T-00
- A deniable paramilitary team hits Umbrella's black site in the Nevada desert (a Shop facility disguised as a data center).
- ▲ **First time the Tyrant is used on human beings.** Horror sequence in the dark. Afterwards: no bodies.
- The argument: Adolf — "An army of these." Wesker — "An army of these is a war. We are ready for a market."

### 22. Foreign Eyes
- MI6's Julian Ashcombe; the BND; the DGSE; MSS; FSB. Anomalous patents, missing mercenaries, the ghost video.
- A London visit: a Downing Street life-sciences investment reception (the PM in a brief cameo handshake). Ashcombe meets Catherine and leaves a card.
- Beijing: Director Wen Shulan orders a file opened.

### 23. Carrick Breaks
- Carrick, suspended, breaks into the campus with Haddad's help. He reaches the Sublevel. He sees the Tyrant.
- Wesker is waiting. He doesn't kill him. He ruins him: bribery evidence, a planted account, a career ended by morning.
- ✎ Carrick is fired. He does not break. He hardens.

### 24. The Asset
- Sloane offers a deal: black contracts for the US government in exchange for protection.
- Wesker accepts. The hunt ends; the hunters now work for him. ◆ Level 40. **Viral Dominion I**.
- End of Movement III: "The agencies had been beaten the way a chess player wins. Later they would be beaten the way a disaster wins."

## MOVEMENT IV — CORPORATE CONQUEST (2031 – 2033)

### 25. Halvorsen Pike
- The mega-pharma that paid for the break-in. CEO Ingrid Halvorsen, formidable. Poison pills; the Delaware Court of Chancery; a proxy fight.
- Boardroom Gravity in full effect. Win. ◆ Points flood.

### 26. Aegis
- Aegis Dynamics: drones, munitions, government contracts. Gen. Thomas Wyatt: partner, then prey.
- Wyatt "vanishes". ✎ Evelyn's Progenitor work claims its first volunteer. Wyatt is seen again at a shareholder meeting, changed, smiling too long.

### 27. Lumen
- Sebastian Crane builds an AI to model Umbrella's rise; it finds something system-shaped; he tries to replicate it.
- Wesker lets the model watch him and feeds it a poisoned pattern. Lumen's bet collapses. Crane: "What are you?" ◆ **Thought Acceleration II**.

### 28. Tianhe
- Shenzhen, Hong Kong, Singapore. Tianhe Genomics and the MSS. An espionage chase; a data center fire; a rooftop.
- Wen and Wesker meet for the first time. Mutual recognition.

### 29. The Market Tilts
- Umbrella passes the trillion mark. Company towns. The Umbrella Foundation.
- Walter's funeral; Wesker comes. ✎ Catherine learns Wyatt's fate and gives Ashcombe what she knows. Wesker knows. He lets the channel run. "Everyone betrays. The trick is to own the route their betrayal travels."

### 30. Sixty Thousand
- The Umbrella Congress: a stadium, 60,000 employees and families. LED walls, drones, a march.
- ▲ **The first time a crowd chants for him.** Adolf's sermon at full power in a modern production. Adolf is overwhelmed; Wesker is chilled by how easy it was.
- ◆ Level 60. **Banner-Sense**. The world press: "Cult or company?" End of Movement IV.

## MOVEMENT V — THE VISIBLE KING (2034 – 2046)

### 31. Pale Horse
- A pandemic. Umbrella's vaccine is ready in eleven days. Millions saved.
- ✎ The truth: Evelyn engineered it without orders. Wesker's fury — the loss of control — and his admiration. Adolf approves. Haddad, now WHO, suspects.

### 32. Arklay Free Territory
- ◆ **Oceanic Arcology**. A sovereign city in international waters. The Umbrella Security Service becomes visible.
- Citizens. Fanaticism as a Point engine. Catherine walks the city and sees the eyes. The moral stain.

### 33. Basel
- The Basel Compact: Umbrella becomes the effective government of the world's pharma capital through votes, contracts, and dependence.
- MI6's assassination plan with Catherine's help. It fails in the most aura-farming way: he stands in the smoke and asks if the meeting can continue.
- ✎ Catherine is not killed. She is kept. That is worse.

### 34. Lantern
- The Coalition: Joint Task Force LANTERN — Carrick, Haddad, Ashcombe, Wen's liaison, Belov.
- ◆ Tyrant Cohort; U.S.S. regiments. The first open battle at the Nevada site. The world sees B.O.W.s on live television.

### 35. The Quiet War
- Umbrella withholds Aeterna and the Pale Horse boosters. Populations turn on their governments. Cyberwar. Cities choose.
- Horror and awe: the war is fought with dependence.

### 36. Washington
- Carrick's last stand. The Oval Office (a fictional 2040s president). "Shall we continue the meeting?"
- ✎ Carrick's end.

### 37. The Long Table
- Beijing: Wen bargains, intelligently. Moscow: Belov gambles on a missile.
- ◆ **Aegis Lattice**, the first orbital purchase. The missile never arrives. The world understands.

### 38. The Chant
- 2046. Geneva. The Accord. A crowd of millions, and billions on screens.
- The Umbrella Directorate: humanity under one will. ▲ Wesker and Adolf speak in unison for the first time.
- ◆ Level 100. The Shop grows a new tab: **BEYOND**. End of Movement V.

## MOVEMENT VI — THE SKY (2047 – 2091)

### 39. Dock One
- ◆ Orbital Dock, void-cutter hulls, vacuum and radiation immunities. The Moon, Mars, the Belt in a decade.
- A signal from outside.

### 40. The Tollhouse
- First contact: the Tollhouse Compact, a mercantile polity, treats humanity as a market, then sees the fleet.
- A negotiation aboard the flagship. Wesker aura-farms an alien.
- The Veth Hegemony sends an ultimatum.

### 41. The Hegemony
- War with the Veth. A Veth admiral's point of view: "They do not broadcast. Their ships feel wrong."
- ◆ **Viral Dominion III**. Boarding by Tyrants. Worlds unmade with silence and fire.

### 42. The Choir and the Myriad
- The Choir declares him a plague. The Myriad cannot be frightened, until Viral Dominion teaches it.
- ✎ Evelyn, the Progenitor Matriarch, becomes something that frightens the summoned. Her end.

### 43. Peer or Plague
- ▲ The Veth sue for terms as equals; the Choir names him the Plague. ◆ **Immunity: Memetic Panic**.
- Catherine dies, old, on Earth: the last person who remembers the building. Her last words to him.

### 44. The Banner
- The building in Wilmington, preserved.
- The final bridge. One will. The stars learn what a banner is.
- Last line and final image.
