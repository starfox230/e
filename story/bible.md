# UMBRELLA — Story Bible

**Working title:** *What If Hitler Was Reborn as Albert Wesker… With a System to Build Umbrella?*
**Format:** ~11h45m dramatized YouTube What-If epic (under YouTube's 12-hour upload cap). A narrator, a small cast of character voices, on-screen System panels, scene images, and a sound and music bed in the *Chrysalis* audiobook tradition: sound where it adds immersion, silence where the voice carries.

---

## 1. Tone contract

- Cold, theatrical, predatory, intelligent. Humor is dry and cruel, never sitcom.
- Wesker never looks like he is trying. Rooms go quiet. He arrives last, says less, lets other men finish their own sentences into nooses.
- **The narrator is not his fan.** The narrator is awed the way one is awed by a hurricane on radar. The horror is always visible at the edge of the frame. The story is a villain's ascent told as a tragedy for everyone else.
- Horror scales: a lab that should not exist → a loyalist who should not blink → a video frame that should not be possible → a stadium that chants → a planet that goes quiet → a star system that learns fear.
- Dialogue is sharp and sparing. No speech runs longer than it has to, except the sermons, which are the point.

### Guardrails (my own)
- Adolf is portrayed as the monster history recorded. His remembered past is referenced as crimes ("the war he began, the camps, the millions") — never detailed, never justified, never softened. No Nazi symbols, salutes or ideology is presented as attractive. His 2026 obsession is *order, hierarchy and rule over the whole species*, and the story treats that as the horror it is.
- Real sitting leaders (Trump, Burnham, Macron, Merz, Carney, Takaichi, Meloni, Zelenskyy, Putin) exist as world texture: named in news, briefings, and at most brief non-defamatory cameos. They are never shown committing crimes or being harmed. After ~2033 leaders are fictional successors.
- Real agencies appear as institutions; every agent, officer and official in them is fictional.
- Rival companies are **real-world-style fictional firms** (Verdant Axis, Halvorsen Pike, Aegis Dynamics, Lumen, Tianhe Genomics). Real giants are only mentioned in passing.
- All biology is fictional (Prototype Virus, Progenitor lineage, "Pale Horse"). No real-world procedures, agents, or methods.

## 2. The Man

**Public name:** Dr. Albert Wesker, Director of Research, Umbrella Research Division Seven (Wilmington, Delaware). The System placed him into the paperwork: a PhD from a Swiss institute that closed in 2019, a career of short contracts in Europe, a US work visa that renewed itself. Every record holds up to a casual check. Nothing holds up to a deep one. (Plot fuel for Movement III: Carrick finds that his past is *too clean*.)

**Body:** Tall, blond, swept-back hair, black sunglasses (indoors when he wants the myth), long black coat, black gloves on cold days. Voice like a closed door. Unhurried walk. Carries the **Prototype Virus** at wake-up: burst speed that reads as teleportation, strength enough to fold a car door, reflexes that watch a muzzle flash like a still photograph, healing in minutes, slit pupils that glow amber-red when emotion spikes. He knows all this before the System ever opens a shop. The arrogance is earned.

**Mind — one skull, two engines:**
| | Wesker | Adolf |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Scalpel and mask | Will and sermon |
| Wants | Deniability, patents, stock, black sites, a world that signs before it reads | Banners, loyalty, spectacle, a people that *believes* |
| Voice on page | Clipped, amused, surgical | Low, historical, contemptuous of weakness; speaks rarely, in the quiet |
| Fear | Losing control of the variables | Being forgotten; being small; chaos |
| Contempt for | Stupidity | Weakness, committees, "managed decline" |

- In the script, Adolf speaks as **ADOLF:** (an interior voice — deeper, reverberant). He is never a second protagonist. He surfaces on drives home, in the mirror, in sermons, and in plans that are historically literate in their cruelty.
- **Thesis learned in Chapter 1:** *"You lost because they saw you coming."* Wesker's methods in the service of Adolf's end state.
- **They do not heal. They fuse.** Friction spikes at thresholds:
  1. The first killing that cannot be hidden (Ch 19)
  2. The first time a summoned Tyrant is used on human beings (Ch 21)
  3. The first time a crowd chants for him (Ch 30)
  4. The first time an alien empire treats him as a peer, and another treats him as a plague (Ch 43)
  By Chapter 38 the interior voice speaks in unison with him. By Chapter 44 there is no ADOLF line at all: one will.
- **Ultimate goal, never abandoned:** rule all of humanity. Not money, not fame. Rule. The stars are only the means.
- **Aura-farming toolkit:** arriving last; silence; the removal of the glasses at the exact moment a threat becomes personal; standing unharmed where he should be dead and asking if the meeting can continue; knowing the names of people's children; letting a subordinate take credit in public and owning them in private.

## 3. The System

A private interface only he perceives. It has **no name** and is never given one. Visually: thin, cold, white-and-crimson glass panels with a hairline border; a soft three-note chime; text that types itself. It speaks in a flat feminine register (SYSTEM voice) and says only what is on the panel.

### Stats (10 = average adult human, 20 = human peak, 30+ = superhuman)
| Stat | Start (L1) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | 34 | Prototype baseline |
| Speed | 38 | Burst speed reads as teleportation |
| Intelligence | 22 | Planning, pattern recognition, legal warfare, System optimization |
| Defence | 26 | Resistance to trauma |
| Health | 30 | Regeneration, stamina |
| Charisma | 24 | Adolf's oratory made operational |
| Special | 6 | Multiplier for acquired abilities |

- Each level: **+5 SP** and a Points bonus (level × 100 early; scaling later).
- **Viral Stability** (Movement I only): starts at **71% and decaying ~1% per day**. At 40% the Prototype begins to eat him. His first Points must buy stability. This humbles him exactly once.

### How Points are earned
Corporate conquest; territorial conquest; raising morale or fanaticism among his personnel or ruled populations; acting intelligently (brilliant schemes, perfect timing, turning an investigation into an asset); overcoming enemies. *The System rewards will made real.*

### Gacha
- One pull: 1,000 Points (Movement I). Ten-pull: 9,000. The price scales in later "banners" (named pools that appear at thresholds).
- Rarity: **Common** (grey) · **Rare** (blue) · **Epic** (violet) · **Legendary** (gold) · **Progenitor** (black with a crimson edge; appears from Movement III).
- A bad roll is still useful. A spectacular roll scares even him for half a second, then becomes an addiction. Adolf calls the Gacha *fate*. Wesker calls it *variance*.

### Summoned personnel
Absolutely loyal, incapable of disobedience. Perfect posture, instant obedience, eyes that never search for an exit. They blink less than people should. Cameras struggle with some of them. Human staff find them wonderful at first and wrong later. **Umbrella's inner spine is not HR. It is owned souls.**

## 5. Factions

**Earth**
1. **Umbrella** — Division Seven (Wilmington) → Umbrella Life Sciences (public: vaccines, longevity, biosecurity, contract research) + the black interior (Arklay Sublevel, Progenitor Initiative) → Umbrella Security Service → the Umbrella Directorate.
2. **Domestic regulators and investigators** — FDA-adjacent inspectors, CDC, FBI (Philadelphia field office, WMD Directorate), HHS politics.
3. **Press and conspiracy ecosystem** — Hannah Price; podcasters; "Umbrella Watch" forums. Wrong about the details, right about the smell.
4. **Rival pharma and defense** — Verdant Axis, Halvorsen Pike, Aegis Dynamics, Lumen, Tianhe Genomics.
5. **Foreign services** — MI6 (Ashcombe), BND, DGSE, MSS (Wen), FSB (Belov). They notice anomalous patents, missing persons, and impossible security footage.
6. **The Coalition** (from Ch 34) — Joint Task Force LANTERN.

**The Sky (Movement VI)**
- **The Tollhouse Compact** — mercantile; many species; treats every new people as a market. First contact. Misjudges humanity once.
- **The Veth Hegemony** — imperial; a caste empire older than Earth's oceans; sees Wesker as a peer, then as a rival emperor.
- **The Choir of Ashen Light** — theocratic; hears the universe as a hymn; declares him a plague.
- **The Myriad** — a hive; the only enemy that cannot be frightened, until Viral Dominion teaches it.
- *Horror note:* his ships feel wrong even to species who have seen war. They do not broadcast. They do not negotiate. The crews are summoned or converted and never break. Boarding is done by Tyrants. Worlds are unmade with silence and fire. Human fanaticism exported into vacuum.

## 6. Places

- **Division Seven** — a four-storey brick research building in an industrial park on the south bank of the Christina River, Wilmington, Delaware. Faded blue sign. A basement with a "new HVAC unit". The break room with a humming vending machine. Later a shrine.
- **The Arklay Sublevel** — BSL-4 beneath the campus, reached by a freight elevator that officially goes nowhere.
- **Umbrella Campus** — the industrial park, bought building by building.
- **Arklay Free Territory** — an ocean arcology (Ch 32).
- **Basel** — pharma capital; site of the Compact and the assassination attempt (Ch 33).
- **Orbital Dock "Spencer's Rest"** — no. Just **Dock One**. Wesker refuses sentiment in names, except once.

## 8. Audio/visual language

- **Music:** dark orchestral. Low strings and piano for Wilmington. Pulse and synth for markets. Choir and brass for sermons. A march for the stadium. Cold, wide pads for space. The leitmotif is a descending four-note figure (the Umbrella motif), first heard on a detuned piano in the empty lab.
- **Sound:** rain on brick, fluorescent hum, freezer compressors, keyboards, phones, crowds (murmur → cheers → chants), gunfire, impacts, sirens, the cryo-cradle's breathing, the System chime, ship hum, alien resonance. Sparse, like *Chrysalis*: effects punctuate, they never wallpaper.
- **Images:** cinematic, desaturated, teal-and-amber for Earth; red-and-white for Umbrella; crimson and black void for the Sky. Wesker is always partly in shadow.
- **System panels:** on-screen glass windows with typed text. The chime fires first; the voice follows.
