## Description

September 2026. A man wakes up in a leather chair in a dying laboratory in Wilmington, Delaware,
with rain on the window, a virus in his blood, and two lifetimes of memory fighting over one skull.

One of them ended in a bunker in 1945. The other ended in a volcano, in a world that never happened
here. Nobody on this Earth has heard the name Albert Wesker. Nobody has heard of Umbrella.

He has seven days to take control of a company with six employees that cannot make its payroll —
and a System only he can see that will pay him, in Points, for every act of conquest, every convert,
and every enemy overcome.

This is the complete story, from a break room with a broken piano to a fleet that teaches the stars
what a banner is. 50 chapters. Six movements. No skipped rungs on the ladder.

⚠️ A note on what this is: a villain's ascent, told as a tragedy for everyone else. The man at the
centre is a monster in both of his lives, and the story never pretends otherwise. His historical
crimes are named and never justified. Every official, agent, scientist, rival company and alien
polity in it is invented.
