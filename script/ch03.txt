N: The blueprint used heat and a chaotropic salt. Everybody used heat and a chaotropic salt.

N: He spent four nights on it, alone, with the vault open and a rack of the archive's stranger cell lines thawing beside him, and on the fourth night he cut the reaction to nineteen minutes by doing something that no competitor's chemist would have dared put in a regulatory filing: he stopped trying to open everything at once.
N: Two chambers instead of one.
