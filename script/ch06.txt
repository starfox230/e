N: The narrator will now tell you what this actually looked like from the inside of a normal human life, because this is the part of the story that people always skip and it is the part that matters.

N: This is how it went, every time, for sixty-five years. Not one person was fooled by an argument. They were simply handed an unbroken record, and left alone with it, and the human mind does the rest without being asked.
[img] a young man flying a camera drone by a river at dusk, laptop open on a car bonnet, city skyline
N: There was one exception, and he was nineteen.

N: He went quiet for four years. He comes back in 2031, and by then he is very good at this, and by then it does not matter at all.
